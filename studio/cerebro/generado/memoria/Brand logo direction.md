# Brand logo direction

> Author's approved brand decisions for Veta de Fortuna — logo is a pixel-art miner's coin, softer text, tagline and subtitle chosen; what they rejected

Approved on 2026-10-12 (docs/13, assets in `studio/assets-ref/marca/`):
- **Logo:** pixel-art coin with a brass ring; inside, a miner's hard hat with a round lamp, three simple Aetherite shards (wine red, thin cyan outline) in the lens, and three stepped bars (trade chart). The icon must work alone and big with the name below.
- **Name lettering:** chunky pixel art in a muted pale steel blue, NOT pure white.
- **Tagline:** «Todo tiene precio. Hasta la esperanza.» **Subtitle:** «El éxito se gana a pico y pala» / «Success is earned with pick and shovel». **Pixel font candidate:** Pixelify Sans ([[F1-18 Fuente pixel con caracteres latinos extendidos y plan de|F1-18]] verifies license).
- **Rejected:** formal/flat vector logos, diamond-cut gems for the ether, busy detail and sparkles on the crystal, a vein pattern over the letters, white lettering.

**Why:** the author wants the brand to feel like the video game (pixel art), simple and readable at small size, and to show lamp + ether + commerce at once.

**How to apply:** keep any new brand piece pixel-art and minimal; never put the ether (wine/pink + cyan) loose on a healthy character, only inside a sealed capsule or on La Fosa mutants; for hard-to-get art use the smarter model `nano_banana_2_1` with thinking high (the cheaper gpt_image_2_5 drifted on shapes and text).
