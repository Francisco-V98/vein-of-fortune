# Provisional art policy

> All current graphics (characters, minerals, scenes, logo, animations) are provisional references; final art is pixel art made with PixelLab later; fail cheap and fast during tests

Everything graphic the project has today, **including the logo**, characters, minerals, scenes, environments and animations, is **provisional**: a reference or internal test asset. The **final art will be redone in pixel art with the PixelLab tool**, and the same goes for final voices and other real assets, only **after the game has been tested and works well**. Real assets are for final production, not for tests.

**Why:** the author wants to fail cheap and fast. Spending credits or time polishing art that will be replaced is waste; tests and storyboards use cheap provisional images.

**How to apply:**
- Never treat current art as final or spend effort polishing it. Mark it provisional in docs and data.
- For tests, storyboards and prototypes: reuse existing character sheets and minerals as references; scenes, environments and moments are regenerated cheaply for each need (the map may be reused or regenerated: decide per case with the author).
- Schedule real pixel-art assets (PixelLab), voices and final images in the roadmap only at the point where final production needs them, not before.
- Credit control stays: only the coordinator generates images, and only after telling the author the count and cost.
