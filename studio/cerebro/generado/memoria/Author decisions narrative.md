# Author decisions narrative

> Author's confirmed story decisions for Veta de Fortuna (Anselmo, Reme, Minister) and how they like to work on narrative

Confirmed by the author on 2026-10-11 (recorded in studio/docs/08 and 19):
- **Anselmo is alive but believed dead and sick**; he is an accomplice of the Comisión del Éter, discovered by the protagonist at the end of Act IV, leaving threads for sequels/expansions.
- **Reme** betrays in Act II by obligation (not by choice) and by the end of Act IV becomes the protagonist's closest ally.
- **Minister Montenegro** is the clear enemy: hard, inflexible, defends a reason that is not very reasonable.
- Story acts: zones as in doc 17; employees: first contact at end of Act I, hiring implemented in Act II; black market in Act II; durations in real time and flexible (player can do side contracts).
- The author wants the narrative developed in much more depth (narrative bible, milestone F0.11 in the roadmap) and felt the ending could seem inconclusive.

**Why:** these are canon for all later story work (guiones, árbol de decisiones, finales).

**How to apply:** treat them as ✅ canon; build on them in [[F0-43 Pasado de Valdoria y de la Aetherita|F0-43]]..[[F0-48 Lectura final y coherencia de la biblia narrativa|F0-48]]; never contradict. Related: [[Design cleanliness standard]]
